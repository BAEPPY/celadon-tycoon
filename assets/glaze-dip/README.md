# Glaze dipping game artwork

Generated with the image generation tool on 2026-11-11 to match the existing illustrated celadon game. The uploaded glazing video informed the milky white unfired glaze, immersion, lifting, and drip motion. This is a game timing activity; five seconds is not a universal ceramic glazing recipe.

- `vat-back.webp`: complete vat and white liquid, behind the student-authored pottery.
- `vat-front.webp`: matching near rim and wall, drawn in front of the moving pottery.
- `ripples.webp`: separate white/gray liquid ripples.
- `thin-overlay.webp`: sparse matte wash, clipped and projected onto the actual work.
- `thick-overlay.webp`: broad glaze flows and pulled-away areas, clipped and projected onto the actual work.

Vat layers share a registered crop from their generated canvases. Source images remain in `/workspace/generated_images`; their identifiers and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. Runtime WebP encoding preserves transparency. No generated pottery replaces the student's shape or motifs. Unfired glaze is white; celadon color appears after the second firing.
