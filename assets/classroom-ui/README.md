## Close the Chromebook

The teal laptop has a connected display, hinge and keyboard, with a downward arrow showing the lid closing. Its mint circle matches the classroom waiting screen.

- Generated with `image_gen` on 2026-11-08 at the user's explicit request.
- Reference: the former `closedLaptopIcon` SVG in `classroom-app.js`.
- `close-chromebook.png`: unchanged generated original, 1254 × 1254 RGBA, available for download.
- `close-chromebook.webp`: 512 × 512 RGBA runtime copy, mechanically resized and encoded at quality 96.
- Original SHA-256: `b0a95b150827a23f822b8ac2b5bdc3f0560a6134426f4b7102ae563bb16b551c`.

The outer background is transparent and the mint circle is part of the image. The waiting-screen CSS displays the circular portion, avoiding edge pixels outside the badge. Checked at 96px and 130px and in portrait, landscape and Chromebook layouts.
